Movie 1 - The Wolf of Wall Street

COMEDY/DRAMA

Mac's Movie of the Month!
Read About Movie The Wolf of Wall Street follows Jordan Belfort, a young stockbroker who quickly rises from a beginner on Wall Street to a very wealthy and powerful businessman. He builds a successful company and enjoys a life filled with money, luxury, and excitement. However, his success comes from dishonest and illegal business practices, eventually attracting the attention of the FBI. The movie shows how greed and the desire for more can lead to both incredible success and serious consequences.

★★★★★

Movie 2 - Interstellar

SCI-FI

Read About Movie Interstellar takes place in a future where Earth is becoming harder to live on because crops are dying and food is running out. Cooper, a former pilot and father, joins a team of astronauts on a dangerous mission through a wormhole to search for a new planet where humans can survive. Along the way, they face incredible challenges in space while Cooper struggles with being away from his family. With amazing visuals, action, and an emotional story, Interstellar is an exciting adventure about space, survival, and family.

★★★★★

Movie 3 - Spider Man Brand New Day

ACTION

Read About Movie Spider-Man: Brand New Day follows Peter Parker as he starts a new chapter in his life as Spider-Man. After the events of Spider-Man: No Way Home, Peter is fighting crime on his own in a world where nobody remembers who he is. As he tries to move forward, new challenges and dangerous enemies begin to appear. With action, humor, and plenty of surprises, Peter must figure out what kind of hero he wants to be.

★★★★☆

Movie 4 - Ratatouille

FAMILY

Read About Movie Ratatouille is about a rat named Remy who dreams of becoming a famous chef, even though rats are not exactly welcome in the kitchen. After ending up in a fancy restaurant in Paris, Remy secretly helps a young kitchen worker named Linguini become a better cook. Together, they work to prove that anyone can become great at something if they follow their passion.

★★★★☆

Movie 5 - 10 Things I Hate About You

ROM-COM

Read About Movie 10 Things I Hate About You is a funny romantic comedy about two sisters who have very different personalities. When the younger sister wants to date, her strict father makes a rule that she can’t go out unless her older sister, Kat, does too. A plan is made to get Kat a date, but things become more complicated when real feelings start to develop. With romance, comedy, and plenty of drama, the movie shows that love can happen when you least expect it.

★★★★☆
